Why SmartScape

Built for the yards in our warming world.

Every conventional yard is a climate retrofit waiting to happen — SmartScape guides you through it.

From our collapsing insect and bird populations, to water-thirsty suburban lawns and yards, to the fire risk that comes with flammable non-native landscaping, what we plant in our yards matters more than most people realize.

SmartScape exists for everyone: a hybrid of human expertise paired with AI power that turns "I don't know where to start" into a personalized, native plant list, planting layout, and local landscaper and nursery matches — in minutes, not months.
