Where SmartScape Is Headed

SmartScape starts with residents — yards, balconies, patios, wherever people garden — and the platform is built to scale across residential, commercial, and institutional properties.

Phase 1 — Now

Direct-to-consumer Greenprints: personalized native landscape plans for any yard, matched to region, climate, and site conditions — shaped and directed by human intelligence and expertise, powered by Claude AI. Available today at greenprints.smartscape.co.

Phase 2 — Expansion

  • HOAs and municipalities, bringing native plants into shared and public spaces at scale
  • Green infrastructure — living roofs, living walls, streetscapes, and other urban native-plant installations
  • Institutional campuses — corporate, academic, and faith communities
  • Real estate developers and new construction, building native landscaping in from the start
  • Tribal nations, in partnership rather than as customers
  • New tools: wildfire flammability scoring and plant-health diagnosis
  • A community hub connecting native-plant growers and enthusiasts

Why it scales

Phase 2 customers become paying accounts — HOA and municipal licensing, developer partnerships — making the platform self-sustaining beyond the consumer product.
